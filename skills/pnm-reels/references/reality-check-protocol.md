# REALITY CHECK PROTOCOL v1

**Permanent rule for the Pandits Near Me Reels Engine.**
Added after the PNM-001 the video tool test, 28 September 2026.

This protocol runs **before any prompt is written** and **before a topic is approved**. It is not a production step. It is a gate.

---

## WHY THIS EXISTS — THE PNM-001 FAILURE

The test reel was built around the three "eyes" of a coconut.

**Verified reality:** the three eyes are circular indentations on the **endocarp** — the hard brown inner shell — clustered at **one end** of the nut, with a seam running between two of the eyes to the opposite pole. The fibrous husk must be removed before they are visible. Two are plugged and hard; one is soft (the germ pore), sitting directly over the embryo.

**What the video model generated:** a fully **husked** coconut, coir fibres intact, with three holes in a **horizontal row across the middle of the side**, looking drilled rather than natural.

Three compounding errors in the one object the entire reel was about — including in the cover frame.

**Root cause:** the topic was approved without anyone confirming whether the central object could be rendered accurately. The failure was not in the prompt. It was in allowing the topic through.

---

## PART 1 · THE GENERATABILITY TEST

Run at **topic selection**, in Plan mode, before a topic is offered for approval.

For each candidate topic, answer:

```
[ ] Q1. Does the payload of this reel depend on the precise
        physical appearance of a specific object?

[ ] Q2. Would a wrong detail in that object make the reel
        factually incorrect, rather than just imperfect?

[ ] Q3. Can we obtain a real photograph or real footage of
        that object?

[ ] Q4. Does the reel require hands performing a ritual action
        in close-up?

[ ] Q5. Does the reel require deity iconography in close-up?

[ ] Q6. Does the reel require any Devanagari or Sanskrit
        visible in frame?
```

**Scoring**

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| Q1 = No, Q4-Q6 = No | **APPROVE** — generate freely |
| Q1 = Yes, Q3 = Yes | **APPROVE WITH REFERENCE** — real photo required, image-to-video only |
| Q1 = Yes, Q2 = Yes, Q3 = No | **REJECT** — offer a different topic |
| Q4, Q5 or Q6 = Yes | **APPROVE WITH FILMED FOOTAGE** — those shots are filmed, not generated |

**PNM-001 scored Q1 = Yes, Q2 = Yes, Q3 = Yes.** It should have been flagged APPROVE WITH REFERENCE from the start, and no shot of the coconut should ever have been text-to-video.

## PART 2 · THE THREE OBJECT TIERS

Every object appearing in a reel is classified before prompting.

### TIER 1 — Generate freely, text-to-video

Generic and atmospheric. A wrong detail is invisible or harmless.

- Home interiors, walls, floors, doorways, windows
- Cloth, curtains, cushions, folded fabric
- Flowers, petals, leaves, foliage
- Light, shadow, smoke, dust motes
- Weather, sky, water, outdoor environments
- Generic temple architecture at a distance
- People in wide shot with no ritual action

### TIER 2 — Generate ONLY with a real reference image

Specific and recognisable. A wrong detail is a factual error your audience will spot.

- Coconut — **husked vs de-husked, eye position, seam**
- Kalash, lota, brass vessels — proportions, mouth, base
- Thali contents and arrangement
- Diya shape and placement
- Mauli / kalava — thread colour and winding
- Havan kund — shape by purpose
- Samagri items
- Toran, rangoli patterns
- Janeu placement
- Tilak pattern by sampradaya
- Specific mala types and strand counts

**Process for Tier 2:** photograph the real object yourself, upload it to the video tool as a reference/ingredient, and use **image-to-video**. Never text-to-video. Never rely on a web image — see Part 3.

### TIER 3 — Never generate. Film it.

The model fails reliably, and the failure is the kind that damages trust.

- Close-up hands performing any ritual action — aarti, abhishek, offering, tying
- Mudras of any kind
- Deity faces and iconography in close-up
- Any Devanagari or Sanskrit script in frame
- Fire in a havan with hands near it
- Any object where the reel's payload IS the fine detail
- Pouring, sprinkling, scattering — physics failures

**Process for Tier 3:** phone, vertical, natural window light. Ten minutes. Always works.

## PART 3 · HOW TO VERIFY — IN ORDER

**Never verify visual facts from an AI model's knowledge. Never verify them from a single website.**

The coconut case proves the point: sources contradict each other on where the eyes sit relative to the stem end. If web sources disagree on a fact this simple, they cannot be the reference for anything subtler.

Verification hierarchy, strongest first:

```
1. THE PHYSICAL OBJECT
   Buy it. Photograph it. Front, back, both ends, close detail.
   Cost: under ₹100. Certainty: total.

2. YOUR OWN PANDIT
   For ritual arrangement, orientation, sequence, sampradaya
   variation. He handles these objects daily.

3. YOUR OWN PUJA PHOTOGRAPHS
   Real ceremonies your pandits have performed.

4. MULTIPLE INDEPENDENT AUTHORITATIVE SOURCES
   Only when 1-3 are impossible, and only when they agree.
   If they disagree, the topic is REJECTED, not guessed.

5. AI MODEL KNOWLEDGE
   Never. Not for visual facts. Not once.
```

**Build a reference photo library.** Every Tier 2 object, photographed once, stored permanently, reused in every reel. One afternoon of work with about ₹2,000 of puja samagri gives you a permanent asset that removes this entire class of failure.

## PART 4 · MANDATORY FLOW SETTINGS

These were violated in the PNM-001 test and caused three of the six failures.

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Audio generation** | **OFF** on every shot | Lip sync will always look wrong. Narration is one clean the editor track. |
| **Character speech** | **None** | No character speaks on camera in any AI-generated shot. |
| **Camera gaze** | **Never direct** | Direct gaze plus imperfect sync is what makes content read as AI. |
| **Shots** | **One prompt per shot** | Never one prompt for the whole reel. the video model will compress it into 8 seconds. |
| **Aspect** | **True 9:16, no letterbox** | Check for black bars on every export. |
| **Watermark** | **Must be removed** | The "the video model" mark appeared in the test. Check your the video tool tier. |
| **Tier 2 objects** | **Image-to-video only** | Text-to-video for a specific object is how PNM-001 failed. |

## PART 5 · CORRECTED PNM-001 SHOTS

**Status: this reel is downgraded to APPROVE WITH REFERENCE + FILMED FOOTAGE.**

Before regenerating anything:

```
[ ] Buy 2 coconuts — one with husk, one de-husked
[ ] Photograph the de-husked nut: the eye end straight on,
    at 45 degrees, the seam, and a scale shot
[ ] Confirm which eye is soft by pressing each one
[ ] Upload the eye-end photograph to the video tool as a reference image
```

### Shots that change

**SHOT 1 — TITLE FRAME.** Now **image-to-video from your own photograph.** Do not describe the coconut in text at all.

```
Using the attached reference image of the coconut's eye end.

Extremely slow push-in on the reference object. No movement of
the object itself. The three pores remain sharp and centred
throughout. Brass plate beneath, softening out of focus.

Light: single window from camera-left, late morning, warm.
Camera: 85mm macro, locked off, minimal push-in only.

Do not alter the object's shape, surface, pore positions or
pore spacing. Reproduce the reference exactly.

Negative: added holes, moved holes, holes in a row, holes on
the side, fibrous husk, drilled appearance, carved symbols,
text, letters, script, hands
```

**SHOT 3 — HANDS PLACING COCONUT.** Reclassified **TIER 3. Film it.** Phone, vertical, window light, real brass kalash, real coconut, 6 seconds, trim to 4.

**SHOT 5 — THE THREE MARKS.** Now **image-to-video from your own photograph.**

```
Using the attached reference image of the coconut's eye end.

The object rotates very slowly on its vertical axis, no more
than 25 degrees of total rotation across 4 seconds. The three
pores stay clearly visible throughout. Dark neutral background.

Light: single warm key upper-left, gentle rim light right.
Camera: 100mm macro, locked off.

Reproduce the reference object's surface and pore layout
exactly. Do not invent additional markings.

Negative: full rotation, fast spin, added holes, holes in a
row, drawn eyes, face-like appearance, carved symbols,
fibrous husk, text, morphing, warping
```

**SHOT 6 — THUMB ON THE SOFT EYE.** Reclassified **TIER 3. Film it.** Phone macro, real coconut, real thumb, 6 seconds, trim to 4.

**SHOTS 4 and 8 — PANDIT_PRIMARY.** Regenerate with audio OFF and no speech. Add to the negative prompt:

```
speaking, mouth moving, talking, direct gaze into camera,
lip movement, wardrobe change, missing angavastram,
altered tilak, bright yellow kurta
```

Confirm in both shots: saffron kurta (not bright yellow), angavastram over the **left** shoulder, three-strand rudraksha, and an identical forehead marking. Place the two shots side by side before accepting either.

### Shots unchanged

Shots 2 and 7 are Tier 1. Regenerate as originally written, with audio OFF.

## PART 7 · THE STANDING RULE

> **If the reel's payload is a physical detail, that detail is photographed, not generated.**
>
> **If it cannot be photographed and sources disagree, the topic is rejected.**
>
> A rejected topic costs one line in Plan mode.
> A published reel with wrong ritual anatomy costs trust you cannot buy back.
