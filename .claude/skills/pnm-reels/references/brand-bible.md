# BRAND BIBLE

Permanent rules. These do not change per reel.

---

## 1 · LANGUAGE SPEC

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Spoken narration | Hindi |
| On-screen title cards | Devanagari |
| Subtitles | Devanagari, **typed from the approved script** — never auto-transcribed |
| Numbers, dates, prices | Latin digits — `500+`, `11 अक्टूबर 2026`, `₹2,100` |
| Brand name | `Pandits Near Me` in Latin, always |
| Sanskrit terms | Devanagari on screen; **never voiced by TTS** |
| Roman-Hindi / Hinglish | Not permitted on screen |
| Caption exception | One Roman keyword line at the end of captions, for Instagram Search |

Mixed-script lines such as `Pandits Near Me पर बुक करें` are correct — the brand name is the single Latin exception.

### Typography

| Use | Font |
|---|---|
| Title bar / title card | Khand Bold or Poppins Devanagari Bold |
| Subtitles | Poppins Devanagari SemiBold |
| Display / covers | Rozha One (optional) |

Devanagari carries matras above **and** below the baseline. Line height 1.35–1.4, never the Latin default. Test every conjunct (क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ) at phone size before export.

### Why Hindi-only

Buys volume and the largest Indian audience. **Costs** the NRI segment, where ARPU runs roughly 10× domestic, and serves Chennai, Thiruvananthapuram and Visakhapatnam poorly. A deliberate trade. Revisit as a second account later, not a change to this one.

## 2 · CONTENT ETHICS — NON-NEGOTIABLE

### Never

- Invent a religious fact, a scripture quotation, or a muhurat
- Promise a spiritual or material outcome — no "this puja will cure", "will fix your marriage", "will bring wealth"
- Use fear to sell — no "if you don't do this, something bad will happen"
- Misrepresent a ritual, a vidhi, or a sampradaya's practice
- Make unsupported astrological claims
- Reproduce mantras or stotras beyond a short verified fragment
- Name or disparage a competitor
- Copy another creator's reel, structure or footage
- Repost anyone else's content — originals get materially more distribution, and accounts posting 10+ reposts in 30 days are excluded from recommendations entirely

### Always

- Respect the sanctity of the subject. Devotees are watching, not just search traffic.
- Use respectful language for Pandit Ji throughout
- Make tradition legible to a modern audience without flattening it
- Lead with value; let the brand emerge as the answer to "so how do I actually get this done properly?"
- Say "it varies by region or family tradition" when that is the truth — it is a stronger and more shareable answer than a fabricated rule
- Route every factual claim through the pandit before shooting

### Deity policy

Deities **may** be rendered fully. In exchange:

- Every deity frame gets a **second review pass**, separate from the general accuracy check
- **No deity frame ships without the pandit's explicit sign-off**
- Check every time: arm count, attribute in each hand, vahana, consort, skin tone by convention, Ganesha's broken tusk and trunk direction
- Reverent contexts only. No modern settings, no comedic or casual framing, no humanised gestures.

## 3 · CTA ARCHITECTURE

**There is no instant checkout.** The funnel is lead → confirmation call. Every CTA drives WhatsApp, DM or the form. **Never "book now".**

| Surface | CTA |
|---|---|
| In-reel bar (from 0:08) | `कुंडली दिखाने के लिए WhatsApp करें: (+91) 97246-76277` |
| End card (final 4s) | Full contact card — phone, email, website, address |
| Narration close | Soft, identity-based. Never salesy. |
| Caption | Service-page mention + **an explicit send request** |

### The send request is mandatory

Sends carry several times the weight of likes for reaching non-followers, and with ~476 followers essentially all reach comes from strangers. Temple Girl asks for the share in words, every time. So do we.

Pattern: `उस इंसान को भेजिए जिसके घर अगले महीने गृह प्रवेश है 🙏`

**Build for the family WhatsApp forward, not the like.**

## 4 · CONTENT MIX

Per month, 8 reels:

| Type | Count |
|---|---|
| Evergreen curiosity | 5 |
| Calendar / festival | 2 |
| Promotional | 1 |

**Evergreen is the engine; festivals are the accelerant.** Of the 12 reference reels analysed, only half were festival-tied — and the sharpest hooks in the set had no date attached at all.

Four reasons this matters for a two-person team: evergreen has no deadline so nothing gets rushed through review; it batches months ahead; it earns views for years where a festival reel dies in eight days; and on a festival day you compete with every devotional account posting the same thing.

**Keep a buffer of 3–4 finished evergreen reels on the shelf at all times.** Pandit rejects a script two days before Navratri? Pull one off the shelf. No gap, no panic, nothing unverified shipped because the calendar said so.

## 5 · BANNED PHRASES

**In hooks:** see `hook-formulas.md`.

**In narration and captions:**
- क्या आप जानते हैं — dead opener
- आइए जानते हैं / बने रहिए / अंत तक देखिए
- इस भागदौड़ भरी ज़िंदगी में
- गारंटी / निश्चित रूप से / 100% — any certainty claim about outcome
- चमत्कार / जादू applied to a service
- Any phrasing that implies harm from *not* booking

## 6 · DISTRIBUTION FACTS THAT SHAPE THE WORK

- **Sends and watch time drive reach.** Likes barely matter.
- **Watch time counts replays** — a 15s reel watched three times beats a 60s watched once. But story sustains attention differently; the reference set runs 45–178 seconds successfully.
- **Hashtags are capped at 5 and don't improve reach.** Caption keywords do the work, because Search reads captions, on-screen text and audio.
- **~65% of Indian Reels are watched on mute.** Subtitles are not optional on any reel.
- **Instagram is weighting raw, real, human content over AI-generated material.** Filmed footage of a real pandit is now the algorithmically favoured format — and it is the one thing no competitor can replicate.
- **Cover images matter for the profile grid, not the feed.** In feed, reels autoplay — the first 1.5 seconds is the scroll-stopper. At the current follower count, ~95% of the work is the opening second.
