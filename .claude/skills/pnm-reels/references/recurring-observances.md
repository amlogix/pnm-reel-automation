# RECURRING OBSERVANCES

**The evergreen content engine.** These repeat every month or fortnight, forever. They never need calendar verification for *existence* — only the specific date each month.

This is where consistent content lives. A festival comes once a year; Ekadashi comes twice a month.

## WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE FESTIVAL LIST

**Diwali comes once. Ekadashi comes 24 times.**

The 24 Ekadashis each have a distinct name and an origin story — Indira, Papankusha, Rama, Utpanna, Mokshada, Nirjala, Shayani, Prabodhini and so on. That is **24 ready-made Format B narrative reels**, each with a built-in origin-reveal hook, spread evenly across the year.

Same for the 12 Sankrantis, the 12 monthly Shivaratris, the 12 Purnimas.

**Content planning consequence:** you will never have a month with nothing to post. Every month contains at minimum 2 Ekadashis, 2 Pradosh, 1 Amavasya, 1 Purnima, 1 Sankashti, 1 Shivaratri, 1 Sankranti — **9 guaranteed anchor dates.**

## THE RECURRING-ANGLE RULE

A recurring observance can only be used **once per cycle, with a different angle each time.**

Example — Ekadashi over six months:

| Month | Angle | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The origin story of this specific Ekadashi | B |
| 2 | Why no rice on Ekadashi? | D |
| 3 | What if you break the fast by mistake? | D |
| 4 | Why two Ekadashis in a month? | A |
| 5 | Who should not fast? | A |
| 6 | The story of Ekadashi Devi herself | B |

Log each angle in `content-log.csv`. The de-duplication check blocks a repeated *angle*, not a repeated *date*.

## SEASONAL WINDOWS

Longer periods that carry content for weeks, not a day.

| Window | Typical period | Service pages | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pitru Paksha** | 16 days, Bhadrapada Purnima to Mahalaya Amavasya | Full Shradh cluster — 10 sub-types | Highest-density shradh content window of the year |
| **Navratri** | 9 nights, twice yearly (Chaitra + Sharad) | `/navratri` 4 pages, `/puja/ghatasthapana-puja` | Plus 2 Gupt Navratris |
| **Wedding season** | Dev Uthani Ekadashi to Dhanu Sankranti, then Makar Sankranti onward | `/puja/marriage-puja`, `/puja/griha-pravesh-puja`, `/muhurat` | **Peak booking window** |
| **Kharmas** | Dhanu Sankranti to Makar Sankranti (~1 month) | `/muhurat` | No weddings — the "why can't we marry now?" angle |
| **Kartik Snan** | Sharad Purnima to Kartik Purnima (~1 month) | `/puja` | Holy bathing month |
| **Adhik Maas** | Every ~32 months | `/puja` | The extra month — strong curiosity topic |
| **Shravan** | Full month, monsoon | `/puja/rudrabhishek-puja` | Mondays especially |

## PLANNING RULE

> **Festivals are the accelerant. Recurring observances are the engine.**

At 8 reels a month, you need roughly 2 calendar-linked topics. Use a festival when one falls inside the window. Otherwise use that month's Ekadashi, Purnima or Sankashti — they are always there, they always have an untold angle, and nobody else is covering them properly.
