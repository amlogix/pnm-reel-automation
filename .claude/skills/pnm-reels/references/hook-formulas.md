# HOOK FORMULAS

**Extracted by analysis of 12 high-performing devotional reels** (@thetemplegirl, 2M followers, 270 posts; Astro Abhishek Goswami).

The hook is the highest-leverage element in a reel. Get it wrong and nothing else matters.

---

## THE CORE RULE

> **A hook must carry STAKES, TABOO, STRANGENESS or SECRECY.**
> A bare "why do we do X" is informational, not curious. It gets rejected at generation.

Checked against the reference set:

| Hook | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| If a deceased person appears in your dream? | consequence + eerie |
| Ever noticed this half-human, half-lion? | strangeness |
| What happens when you look at the moon? | taboo + consequence |
| Why do Navagrahas **never** face each other? | strangeness + secrecy |
| The **real** story of Rakshabandhan | secrecy |
| 16 powerful names — **before you start anything** | secrecy + use-case |

And the weakest in the set — "why do we do Nimajjan on the 11th day" — is the purely procedural one. Same flaw to avoid.

**Brand-safe use of taboo:** the *question* carries anxiety, the *answer* resolves it calmly with verified fact. That is exactly what a pandit does for a client all day. No fear-mongering needed — the relief is the payload.

---

## THE SEVEN MECHANICS

**1 · Presupposition, not inquiry.** "हम गृह प्रवेश में नारियल क्यों फोड़ते हैं?" doesn't ask *whether* you do it. It assumes you do and quietly asserts you don't know why. An accusation wearing a question's clothes. Compare "गृह प्रवेश का महत्व" — same topic, zero pull, because nothing is claimed about the reader.

**2 · Deixis — the word "this".** "ये धागा क्यों?" only works because something is circled in the frame. Text points at picture, picture needs text. A line that reads fine as plain prose is usually a weak thumbnail line.

**3 · Plain word beats correct word.** Temple Girl wrote "Human Beast", not "Narasimha". The correct term loses both audiences at once — people who know it scroll past, people who don't have no hook. **Translate the technical term into the physical description.** "लाल धागा" over "मौली". "तांबे का लोटा" over "कलश". The correct term goes in the caption, where search reads it.

**4 · Absolutes.** "कभी नहीं", "हमेशा". Checkable and provocative — the brain wants to find the exception. "आमतौर पर" produces nothing.

**5 · Consequence framing.** "क्या होता है अगर…" promises an outcome. Outcomes open loops in a way explanations don't.

**6 · Never leak the answer.** If a reader can guess the answer from the hook, the reel is dead on arrival.

**7 · One specific anchor.** A proper noun or a number — "11वें दिन", "6 बातें", "नवग्रह". Makes it feel factual rather than clickbait, and it's what Instagram Search indexes.

## THE SIX FORMULAS

| ID | Formula | Hindi pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ritual-why` | A ritual you already perform | हम [X] क्यों करते हैं? | हम गृह प्रवेश में नारियल क्यों फोड़ते हैं? |
| `rule-exists` | A rule you didn't know existed | [X] कभी/हमेशा [Y] क्यों? | सत्यनारायण पूजा शाम के बाद क्यों नहीं होती? |
| `consequence` | What happens if | अगर [X] तो क्या होगा? | अखंड ज्योत बुझ जाए तो क्या होता है? |
| `familiar-detail` | Familiar object, unnoticed detail | [X] पर ये [Y] क्यों? | पंडित जी की कलाई पर ये धागा क्यों? |
| `emotional` | Universal emotional experience | [X] ही न हो तो? | अपना गोत्र पता ही न हो तो पूजा कैसे होगी? |
| `authority-asks` | Why the expert asks for something | पंडित जी [X] क्यों पूछते हैं? | पंडित जी आपका गोत्र क्यों पूछते हैं? |

**`authority-asks` is yours alone.** Temple Girl has temples, which anyone can visit. You have a pandit who walks into people's homes and does specific, observable things that everybody has witnessed and nobody has ever asked about. Bottomless, and no competitor can reach it.

## HINDI-SPECIFIC RULES

**Word order changes the design.** Hindi is verb-final, so **क्यों sits late in the sentence**, not at the front like English "WHY". The red/gold highlight therefore lands mid-line or on line two, never on line one's opening word.

**Length:** 5–7 words. Devanagari is denser per character, so the same word count fills more of the box. Test at final size.

**Numbers in Latin digits.** `6 बातें`, not `६ बातें`.

**Strict Devanagari.** No Roman except "Pandits Near Me". Roman transliteration is permitted only on the last line of a caption, for search.

## THE FIVE-POINT TEST

Every hook must pass all five before it ships:

```
[ ] 1. Under 9 words
[ ] 2. Contains आप / आपका / हम / ये  (if not, it's about the
       topic instead of the viewer — rewrite)
[ ] 3. Answer NOT guessable from the line
[ ] 4. One concrete anchor — a number, proper noun, or
       pointed-at object
[ ] 5. Plain word beats correct word
```

Plus the core rule: stakes, taboo, strangeness or secrecy.

## BANNED HOOKS

These are the defaults the account will drift toward. Every one fails the test.

- ❌ `5 फायदे...` / "5 benefits of" — a list, no gap, answer implied
- ❌ `...का महत्व` / "importance of" — abstract noun, no viewer
- ❌ `क्या आप जानते हैं` / "did you know" — dead phrase, signals filler
- ❌ `...समझाया` / "explained" — leaks the answer in the word
- ❌ `वेरिफाइड पंडित बुक करें` — a statement about you, not about them
- ❌ `नमस्ते दोस्तों` — wastes the only 1.5 seconds that matter
- ❌ `आइए जानते हैं` / "let's find out"
- ❌ `बने रहिए` / "stay tuned"
- ❌ `लिंक बायो में` / "link in bio" as a hook
- ❌ `90% लोग ये गलती करते हैं` — works, but only if your pandits can verify the claim. Otherwise it builds distrust at scale. Use rarely and never unverified.

### Rewrite examples

| Banned | Fixed |
|---|---|
| गृह प्रवेश का महत्व | नए घर में सबसे पहले दूध ही क्यों उबालते हैं? |
| रुद्राभिषेक के 5 फायदे | रुद्राभिषेक में दूध ही क्यों, पानी क्यों नहीं? |
| सत्यनारायण पूजा विधि | सत्यनारायण पूजा शाम के बाद क्यों नहीं होती? |
| क्या आप जानते हैं नवग्रह के बारे में | नवग्रह कभी आमने-सामने क्यों नहीं होते? |

Same subject each time. One is a textbook chapter title, the other is a question about the reader's own home.

## THE RULE THAT OUTRANKS ALL OF THIS

> **Every question needs a real answer your pandits will verify before you shoot.**
>
> A great hook with an invented or vague answer costs more trust than a boring hook ever would.

## TWO ADDITIONAL FORMULAS *(added from competitor analysis, Sept 2026)*

| ID | Formula | Hindi pattern | Example | Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `origin-reveal` | The hidden story behind a practice you already perform | [practice] के पीछे की कहानी — [surprise] | पिंड राम जी से नहीं, सीता जी से माँगा था | **B** |
| `practical-doubt` | A question about your own practice with no clear answer anywhere | [practice] कब तक / कितनी देर / क्या सच में? | पूजा कितनी देर करनी चाहिए? | **D** |

### `origin-reveal` — Sri Mandir's engine

Take a practice the audience already performs, reveal its origin story, and make sure the origin contains a surprise.

Their three reels all share this shape: why pind daan at Gaya (Sita made it from sand, four witnesses lied), why Pitru Paksha exists (Karna gave gold all his life but never anna-jal to his own ancestors), why astrology works (it came from meditation).

Your `/ritual/` and Shradh clusters are full of practices whose origin stories nobody tells.

### `practical-doubt` — the unanswered question

Not "what is X" — people can search that. This is **"how much / how long / does it really work"** about their own practice. These have no clear answer online, which is exactly why they get watched, and they lead naturally to "ask a pandit."

पूजा कितनी देर करनी चाहिए? · व्रत अधूरा छूट जाए तो? · क्या रोज़ पूजा करना ज़रूरी है? · पूजा का फल कब मिलता है? · कितने दिन तक हवन करना चाहिए?

## THREE SCRIPT TECHNIQUES FROM COMPETITOR ANALYSIS

**1 · Front-load the reveal — do not save it for the end.**

In the Sita reel the surprising fact lands at **second 5**, in larger type than everything else: "पिंड राम जी से नहीं, सीता जी से माँगा था।" The rest of the reel explains *how*.

This contradicts the usual "save the payoff" instinct. Most reels hold the twist and lose the viewer at second 8. Front-load the surprise and use the explanation as the retention device.

**2 · Hedge every religious claim, every time.**

The Sita script attributes four separate times in 65 seconds: प्रचलित कथा के अनुसार · लोक मान्यता में · मान्यता है कि · माना जाता है.

They never assert a religious fact — they attribute it to tradition or belief. **The market leader treats this as standard practice.** It costs nothing and it is what makes the content defensible.

**3 · Ancient concept, modern analogy.**

An abstract spiritual idea only lands in 30 seconds if it is anchored to something everyday. Reference example: when to stop a practice — *"एक डाउनलोड चलता है ना, पट्टिका घूमती है"*.

**Mandatory in every Format D script.** Analogy bank: a download bar · charging a phone · watering a plant · learning to drive · building muscle · a deposit maturing · a seed germinating. Keep it secular and everyday, and never in a way that diminishes the ritual.
