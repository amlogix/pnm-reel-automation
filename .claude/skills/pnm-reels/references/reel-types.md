# REEL TYPES

**Derived by frame-level analysis of every reference reel supplied.** Four types exist. Each is assigned at topic selection, before any script is written.

**No tool or model is named anywhere in this file.** Models change; the structure does not.

---

## QUICK ASSIGNMENT

| If the topic is… | Type |
|---|---|
| A question, explanation, myth-check, practical doubt | **1 · Split Screen** |
| A story with characters — Puranic, mythological, origin tale | **2 · AI Narrative** |
| An idea, concept or principle with no characters and no speaker | **3 · Narrated Visual** |
| A real recorded conversation | **4 · Podcast Clip** *(needs a recorded session)* |

# TYPE 1 · SPLIT SCREEN

**References analysed:** `Last_Final_1_2X.mp4` (64.7s, 8 segments, AI presenter) · Astro Abhishek Goswami Pitru Paksha (45.5s, 4 cuts, real speaker) · उपवास/हवन reel (86.5s, 2 cuts, real speaker)

### Structure

A speaker occupies one half of the frame. AI visuals run in the other half. A fixed title bar sits between them.

| Act | Layout |
|---|---|
| Hook | **Full frame speaker.** No bar, no split. |
| Body | Split screen |
| Close | **PNM branding screen** |

### Measured geometry — 1080 × 1920

| Zone | Pixels | % |
|---|---|---|
| Speaker panel | 0 – 862 | 0 – 44.9% |
| Title bar | 862 – 1084 | 44.9 – 56.4% |
| AI visual panel | 1084 – 1920 | 56.4 – 100% |

**Speaker is always the top panel.** Never invert — consistency across reels builds format recognition.

Geometry never changes mid-reel. Neither panel is ever blank.

### Observed variants

| Variant | Speaker | Pacing |
|---|---|---|
| AI presenter | Generated | 8 segments, ~8s each |
| Real speaker, mic visible | Filmed | 4 cuts in 45s — nearly static |
| Real speaker, ritual video above | Filmed | 2 cuts in 86s — one continuous take |

**The speaker is rendered as one continuous take wherever possible.** Cuts happen in the visual panel and in framing, not in the speaker's performance. That removes drift entirely.

### Title bar

The strongest continuity device in this type.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Background | Solid black, full bleed |
| Font | Bold Devanagari |
| Base colour | White |
| Highlight | Gold — **the topic keyword only**, usually the first 1–2 words |
| Length | Maximum 7 words, single line |

**The text never changes during the reel.** Constant from the moment the split establishes until the branding screen. It never animates. It is the spine holding the segments together.

### Character handling

**None required.** The speaker is one continuous render. People appearing in the visual panel appear in exactly one segment each and never recur — so no reference images, no matching, no consistency work.

# TYPE 2 · AI NARRATIVE

**References analysed:** Sri Mandir "कर्ण की कहानी" (52.8s, 13 cuts, avg 4.1s, no subtitles, series title card) · Sri Mandir Sita/Gaya Ji (65.4s, 18 cuts, avg 3.6s, Devanagari subtitles + name cards) · `new.mp4` Karna (46s, 8 segments, lead recurs in 5)

### Structure

No speaker on screen. AI-generated characters act out a story. Narration carries it.

| Beat | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 Hook | The question or the claim |
| 2 **Reveal** | The surprise — **delivered at ~second 5, in larger type** |
| 3 Setup | Who, where, what is normal |
| 4 Event | What happens |
| 5 Complication | What goes wrong |
| 6 Resolution | How it ends |
| 7 Bridge | Why it matters to the viewer today |
| 8 **PNM branding screen** | |

**Front-load the reveal.** The Sita reel puts its twist at second 5 — "पिंड राम जी से नहीं, सीता जी से माँगा था" — then spends the rest explaining *how*. This contradicts the instinct to save the payoff, and it is the strongest single technique observed across all references.

### Layout

Full frame throughout. Title bar optional — use it when the story hangs on one framing question.

### Character handling — the hard part

This is where Type 2 lives or dies. Full detail in `character-consistency-protocol.md`.

**Observed in reference:** the lead recurs across five clips. His **face held well**; his **crown and tilak drifted** noticeably between shots. Reference-based consistency locks identity, not ornament.

Therefore:
- Cast sheet locks **face AND costume AND ornament** — every item named or banned
- Shot prompts reference characters **by ID only**, never re-describing them
- **No readable-ornament close-ups** on a recurring character — ornament drift only shows in close-up
- Hero shots are **still-first** — approve the frame before spending on video

**Character budget:**

| Recurring characters | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Ideal |
| 3–4 | Workable with still-first on every hero shot |
| 5+ | Redesign the story |

**Non-recurring characters are free.** In reference footage the lead recurs five times while the couple, the pandit and the family each appear once. Write so only the protagonist recurs.

### Series branding

Reference uses a **theatrical curtain title card** revealing an ornate series name — "कर्ण की कहानी / पितरों का ऋण". That signals an episode of a named series rather than a one-off.

**Worth adopting.** Viewers return for the next episode instead of treating each reel as disposable.

### Subtitles

One reference carries full Devanagari subtitles plus name cards; the other carries none. **Follow the subtitled one.** With most Indian reels watched on mute, no-text is a weakness, not a model.

# TYPE 3 · NARRATED VISUAL

**No speaker, no characters.** Narration over atmospheric and illustrative AI visuals.

### When to use it

Ideas, concepts, principles, practical doubts — anything where nothing needs to be *acted out*.

### Why it matters

**Cheapest type and lowest risk.** No speaker dependency, no character consistency, no deity review. Almost every shot is Tier 1 — nothing in it can render wrong.

### Structure

| Beat | Content |
|---|---|
| Question | The doubt, asked as the viewer would ask it |
| Acknowledge | Say out loud that many people wonder this. Never dismiss it. |
| Analogy | **The modern comparison** |
| Framework | The answer — a principle, not a number |
| Bridge | Pandits Near Me |
| **PNM branding screen** | |

### The script technique — ancient concept, modern analogy

**Mandatory in every Type 3 script.** An abstract idea only lands in 30 seconds when anchored to something everyday.

Reference example: when to stop a practice — *"एक डाउनलोड चलता है ना, पट्टिका घूमती है"*. Completion is the signal, not a fixed count.

Analogy bank: a download bar · charging a phone · watering a plant · learning to drive · building muscle · a deposit maturing · a seed germinating.

Keep it secular and everyday. Never in a way that diminishes the ritual.

**Give a framework, never a fixed number**, unless a verified fixed rule exists.

### Visual vocabulary

All Tier 1. No faces, no hands, no ritual close-ups, no deities.

| Concept | Visual |
|---|---|
| Time passing | sun arc on a wall, shadows moving |
| Completion / cycle | potter's wheel, water filling a vessel, a seed opening |
| Devotion / effort | a steady flame, steps worn by use |
| Doubt / searching | mist over water, an empty doorway, a path into trees |
| Satisfaction | still water, a settled flame, dawn light |
| Scale | mountains, sky, a river from above |

**One visual per idea.** Slower than Type 1 — the narration is carrying the thinking. Visuals illustrate, they do not compete.

### Audio

Music carries the emotional weight here because there is no face and no dialogue. Run it **higher than other types**, and match the mood to the topic — sombre subjects get a drone bed with no percussion.

# TYPE 4 · PODCAST CLIP *(not yet live)*

**References analysed:** Shlloka Yoga telepathy (58.9s, 19 cuts, avg 3.1s) · Mumta Podcast astrology (46.6s, 23 cuts, avg 2.0s)

Real recorded conversation, clipped into a short. **Blocked until a session is recorded.** Documented so the technique is ready.

| Property | Observed |
|---|---|
| Pace | **2.0–3.1s average shot** — roughly double Type 1 |
| Captions | Word-by-word karaoke, boxed |
| Visuals | AI b-roll cut in mid-sentence to illustrate a noun |
| CTA | Persistent bar, lower third |
| Layout | Full frame, or split with the listener below |

## WHAT EVERY TYPE SHARES

- Hindi, strict Devanagari, Latin digits, `Pandits Near Me` in Latin
- **Hook and cover must be a curiosity opener** — see `hook-formulas.md`
- Subtitles always on, typed from the approved script
- One unified colour grade across every shot
- Audio bed unbroken across every cut
- **Final scene is the PNM branding screen** — logo consistent, every reel, no exceptions
- `[VERIFY]` slots stay empty until a human fills them
- Generatability Test passed at topic selection
