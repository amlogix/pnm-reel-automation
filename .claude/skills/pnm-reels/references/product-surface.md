## THE BUSINESS

**Company:** Pandits Near Me — panditsnearme.com
**Positioning:** Where Spirituality Meets Convenience
**Product:** Book verified pandits online for puja, havan and Hindu rituals — in person, or via e-puja over video call, anywhere in India or globally.

**Booking flow:** select puja → details (name, OTP-verified phone, puja name, language, date, time, mode online/offline, samagri needed) → confirmation call → verified pandit assigned.

**This matters for reels:** there is **no instant checkout.** The funnel is lead → call. So every CTA drives WhatsApp, DM or the form — never "book now".

**Priority rule:** puja/havan/ritual booking is the revenue driver. Astrology, horoscope, numerology and panchang exist substantially to build trust that funnels back to puja bookings. Reels default to the puja CTA.
